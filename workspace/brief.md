# Brief: Lumen, a reading-light app

Lumen is a small iPhone app that dims your screen warmer as the evening goes on, so reading before bed is easier on your eyes.

- Price: free, with a $2/month Plus plan (custom schedules, per-app rules).
- Audience: people who read on their phone in bed.
- Tone: calm, plain, a little warm. No hype words.
- Must include: a headline, three short benefits, the price, and one call to action ("Get Lumen").
