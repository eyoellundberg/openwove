# Review: Huila launch copy

Checked every claim in email.md and page.md against company.md and the voice rules (warm, short sentences, no hype words, no health claims).

## Changes to email.md
- Removed "When they're gone, they're gone." company.md says Huila is roasted in small batches of 60 bags. It doesn't say supply is limited or that it won't be roasted again, so this scarcity claim isn't supported.
- Changed "a fresh bag every 2 weeks" to "a bag every 2 weeks". company.md says nothing about freshness.

## Changes to page.md
- Removed "so the sweetness comes through and nothing gets burnt." company.md only says medium roast. It doesn't give a reason for the roast level.
- Removed "That's a small run. Once the bags are gone, they're gone." This is the same unsupported scarcity claim as in the email. "Small batches of 60 bags" stays, since company.md supports it.

## Verified, no change needed
- Launch date Nov 1, Huila (Colombia), medium roast, 12oz, $21, harvest March 2026, batches of 60 bags, tasting notes red apple / brown sugar / cocoa nib: all match company.md.
- Subscription: any bag, every 2 weeks, 10% off. $18.90 is correct ($21 x 0.9).
- Shipping: free over $40, otherwise $5. This is correct. The email leaves out the $5 line, which is fine.
- Refund: 30 days on the first order. This matches "no questions asked on first order."
- No hype words and no health claims in either file.

## Left for a human to decide
- Email: "It's easy to drink and nice to slow down with." This is a subjective description, not a fact or a health claim, so I kept it. Cut it if you want only claims sourced from company.md.
- Page: "Make it a habit" and "Skip the reorder and keep the cup" are marketing voice, not factual claims. I kept them.
