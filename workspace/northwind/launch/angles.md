# Huila single-origin launch angles

Launch date: Nov 1. Source: company.md.

1. **Small batch, small run.** Roasted in batches of 60 bags, so once they're gone, they're gone.
   - Appeals to: people who like to try things before everyone else and who value a limited release.

2. **Harvested Mar 2026, roasted for you.** A Colombian Huila, medium roast, with red apple, brown sugar, and cocoa nib notes.
   - Appeals to: drinkers who want a single origin with clear flavor and a balanced, everyday roast.

3. **Make it a habit.** Subscribe and get 10% off, with a bag every 2 weeks.
   - Appeals to: regular drinkers who already buy a favorite blend and want to add a single origin to the rotation.
